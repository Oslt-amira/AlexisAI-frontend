# Focus companion artwork

Original animated SVG illustrations drawn for Alexis in components/workspace/familiar-art.tsx. Reference images are used only for inspiration.

- Mushroom: a natural, bronze-toned cap with fine gills and a growing shoot; no cartoon face or limbs.
- White cat: a resting ivory silhouette with subtle shading, breathing, a slow tail movement and a nuzzling response.
- Spirit: an elongated pearl form with muted mineral colours, minimal eyes, slow drift and a light response.

The artwork uses restrained movement rather than bouncing, bubbles or hearts. Animation styles live in components/workspace/focus-attachments.css and respect reduced motion. Legacy Twemoji source files remain unused and retain their separate attribution.
